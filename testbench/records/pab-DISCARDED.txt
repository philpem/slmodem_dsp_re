The pab-bug-* / pab-fix-* captures are DISCARDED.  Do not analyse them and do
not cite them.

They are the pre-emphasis A/B (finding 1471 / D53), and they were taken while
three reconstruction agents were compiling.  /proc/loadavg read 10.3 and 11.7
on the two calls that completed, on a 12-core box, and the run was stopped
when it reached 42.

slmodemd is real-time DSP: it must produce and consume 9600 samples a second
on schedule, and scheduling jitter from competing compilers shows up as sample
slips.  That is the exact mechanism that stopped a pjsua conference bridge
carrying a V.34 handshake at all (finding 1468).  A connect failure under that
load says nothing about the branch under test.

The design was right -- interleaved arms, load recorded per call -- and
interleaving does protect against load DRIFT.  It does not rescue a run where
both arms are degraded, because the effect being looked for is smaller than
the degradation.
