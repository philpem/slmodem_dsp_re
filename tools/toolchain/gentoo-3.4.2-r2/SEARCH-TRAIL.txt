CORRECTION (important): my FIRST mirror sweep was methodologically INVALID
=========================================================================
Modern Gentoo mirrors do NOT store distfiles flat. Authority:
  https://distfiles.gentoo.org/distfiles/layout.conf
    [structure]
    0=filename-hash BLAKE2B 8
Files live at  distfiles/<2-hex>/<filename>  where the 2 hex chars are the first
8 bits of BLAKE2B of the FILENAME (computable without having the file):
    1e  gcc-3.4.2-patches-1.1.tar.bz2
    29  gcc-3.4.0-piepatches-v8.7.6.5.tar.bz2
    d3  protector-3.4.1-1.tar.gz
