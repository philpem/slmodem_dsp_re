verify the inventory against the committed batch census. Preserve original
and retained instructions, canonical targets, object/config/census hashes.

observed retained transformations, never claim to measure its compiler state.

classification; do not assign C1/C2 ownership by dump order.

Prior findings must be reviewed before a source-control domain is declared.
Prefer independent use/lifetime/cold-path witnesses and shared implementation
families. Closed singleton controls can be crossed only with a fresh axis.

width/prototype guess, score hill climbing, mutation or fuzzing run. Do not
read re/. Issue22's global profile remains a separate investigation.

must be audited across full TUs, including nonexact bystanders and all defining
copies, then measured in production and gated once at batch end with make phase.
Record misses, excluded/ambiguous mappings, and the next discriminating witness.
